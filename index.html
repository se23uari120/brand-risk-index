Brand Image Risk Index — how it is computed
Risk Index = D+ / (D+ + D−) × 100
FinBERT scores every headline → four criteria C1–C4 (0–100) → weighted by AHP → TOPSIS distance from a fixed lowest-risk baseline (D+) and a fixed maximum-crisis profile (D−). Bands (report Table V): Low < 40 · Moderate 40–<70 · Critical ≥ 70.

Criterion definitions (report Table III, live mode)
CriterionHow the code computes it
s (negative effective sentiment)Recency-weighted mean of FinBERT negative intensity, max(0, −score), over opinionated (non-neutral) headlines, with 2 neutral pseudo-headlines added so one or two items cannot dominate. Weight w = 0.5age / 72h (time decay).
C1 Severityclip[5,100](s × 100)
C2 ReachViral 100 / High 75 / Medium 50 / Low 25, from recency-weighted negative volume and the number of outlets carrying negative stories (Viral ≥ 15 or ≥ 8 outlets; High ≥ 6 or ≥ 4; Medium ≥ 2 or ≥ 2; else Low).
C3 Recoverabilityclip[10,100](0.6·C1 + 0.4·C2)
C4 Criticalityclip[10,100](mk · C1), sector multiplier mk from 0.65 (FMCG) to 1.15 (FinTech).
TOPSIS — step by step
1
Decision matrix
X (m × 4): one row per company, columns C1–C4 on a 0–100 scale, built from that company's FinBERT-scored headlines.
2
Normalisation
The criteria already share a 0–100 scale, so rij = xij / 100. (Classic vector normalisation, x / √Σx², makes every score depend on which other companies are in the set; it is kept as the "relative" mode for comparison.)
3
Weighting
vij = wj · rij with the AHP weights [0.4658, 0.2771, 0.1611, 0.0960].
4
Ideal solutions
All criteria are cost-type. A+ is the lowest-risk baseline (C1 5, C2 25, C3 13, C4 10 — the minimum the Table III formulas can produce) and A− is maximum crisis exposure (100 on every criterion), as described in report Sec. IV-D. Because they are fixed, a searched company and the monitored companies share one scale.
5
Distances
D+ = √Σ(vij − A+j)² (distance from the safe baseline) and D− = √Σ(vij − A−j)² (distance from the crisis profile).
6
Risk index, band and action
Risk Index = D+ / (D+ + D−) × 100. Low < 40 (background logging), Moderate 40–<70 (assign analyst, SEBI check, draft statement), Critical ≥ 70 (executive crisis protocol, legal review, PR response). A 90% confidence range is computed by bootstrapping the headlines.
